Overview

The SB Trident is an independently developed toolhead modification project based on the Voron Stealthburner ecosystem. The goal is to provide a coordinated set of printable parts for builders integrating selected hotend, electronics, sensing and filament-handling components.

Printed parts kit

Designed around functional ASA printed components. Electronics, motors, fasteners and other hardware are not included in the proposed printed-parts kit.

Modular documentation

Assembly steps, a bill of materials, compatibility notes and revision history will be published as each item is checked.

Independent development

Custom CAD work and iterative physical prototypes. The project is not an official Voron Design product.

Kit scope

The planned offering covers 3D-printed plastic components only. The final parts inventory, quantities, colors and revision identifiers will be published before a kit is marked ready for sale. The BOM will separately identify parts that customers must source themselves.
